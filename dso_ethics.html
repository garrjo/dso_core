⚖ Ethics & Risk Mitigation

Responsible development and deployment of seismic forecasting technology

Commitment to Responsible Innovation

The DSO Core Observatory recognizes that seismic prediction carries significant societal implications. We are committed to transparency, scientific rigor, and responsible communication of uncertainty to ensure our technology benefits society while minimizing potential harms.

📜 Core Ethical Principles

🔍
Scientific Transparency

All methodologies, assumptions, and limitations are openly documented and available for peer review.

  • Complete model documentation in the Whitepaper section
  • Open source code for independent verification
  • Regular publication of verification statistics
  • Acknowledgment of model limitations in all communications
📊
Uncertainty Quantification

Every prediction includes rigorous uncertainty estimates. We never present forecasts as deterministic.

  • Confidence intervals accompany all magnitude predictions
  • Probability statements clearly indicate forecast uncertainty
  • Error sources are explicitly identified and quantified
  • Model confidence is distinguished from prediction certainty
⚠
Do No Harm

We actively work to prevent misuse and minimize negative consequences of our forecasts.

  • Forecasts are not intended to replace official emergency management guidance
  • Communication designed to avoid panic or unnecessary economic disruption
  • Clear disclaimers prevent misinterpretation as evacuation orders
  • Collaboration with local authorities before public release

🛡 Mitigation Strategies

  • ✓
    Probabilistic Communication

    All forecasts expressed as probability ranges, not deterministic predictions. Language reviewed to avoid implying certainty.

  • ✓
    Tiered Dissemination

    Technical bulletins to agencies first, simplified public communications second. 24-hour coordination window with emergency management.

  • ↻
    Continuous Verification

    Real-time tracking of prediction accuracy. Automatic alerts if skill scores drop below acceptable thresholds.

  • ✓
    Clear Disclaimers

    Every forecast includes prominent disclaimer about experimental nature and recommendation to follow official guidance.

  • ●
    External Ethics Review Board

    Establishing independent review board with seismologists, ethicists, and emergency management professionals. Target: Q2 2026.

⚠ Known Limitations

🔬
Experimental Technology: The DSO forecasting system is based on novel methodology that has not yet been validated over multiple seismic cycles (decades). Long-term reliability is unknown.
  • Magnitude Precision: Typical prediction range is ±0.5 magnitude units. Individual event magnitudes cannot be predicted exactly.
  • Location Uncertainty: Stress zones are regional (hundreds of kilometers), not point locations. Specific fault identification not possible.
  • Timing: Time windows are typically 20-60 days. Precise event timing (day/hour) cannot be predicted.
  • False Negatives: Not all significant earthquakes will be predicted. The system supplements, not replaces, existing monitoring.
  • Physical Mechanism: The core-mantle coupling hypothesis is theoretical. Causal relationship not definitively established.

📜 Our Commitment

We commit to operating the DSO Core Observatory with the highest ethical standards. Our goal is to contribute to earthquake science and public safety, not to replace established institutions or create undue alarm. We will continuously evaluate our impact, incorporate feedback, and adjust our practices to ensure our work benefits society. If evidence suggests our forecasts cause more harm than good, we will modify or discontinue public dissemination.
