💡 Interpretation

Hit clustering: Notice how hits cluster when combined drag is high (purple shading peaks). The core offset correctly identifies stressed regions, but release requires sufficient drag to push the system over threshold.

Miss patterns: Misses tend to occur when combined drag is low—the fault is primed but threshold isn't reached. These often release later when lunar perigee arrives, or release partially (explaining magnitude underestimates).

~2-year beat: The visible oscillation in the combined drag envelope represents the beat frequency between lunar perigee precession (~8.85 yr) and annual solar perihelion cycle. Constructive interference → higher hit rates.
