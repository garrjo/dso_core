🔗 Core-Mantle Coupling Mechanism

🌍
Core Offset
Inner core displaced from geometric center by ~1-2 km
📈
Asymmetric Pressure
Gravitational potential creates differential stress on mantle
⚠
Stress Zone
Maximum stress accumulates opposite to offset direction

Stress Distribution Formula

σ(θ) = σ₀ + A·cos(θ - θ_offset) + B·cos(2(θ - θ_offset))

Variables

  • σ(θ) - Stress at azimuth θ
  • σ₀ - Background stress level
  • A - Dipole component amplitude
  • B - Quadrupole component
  • θ_offset - Core offset direction

Physical Interpretation

  • Dipole term: Direct gravitational pull from offset mass
  • Quadrupole term: Tidal deformation of outer core
  • Maximum stress at θ = θ_offset + 180°
  • Stress magnitude scales with offset distance

🔄 Differential Rotation

The inner core rotates at a slightly different rate than the mantle, completing an extra rotation approximately every 400-1000 years. This differential rotation, combined with position oscillations, creates time-varying stress patterns at the core-mantle boundary.
