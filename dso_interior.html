⚡ Drag Flow Through Earth's Interior

The Drag-Scale Interior Model

External drag from Sun/Moon doesn't act uniformly on the Earth. It propagates through the interior at rates determined by local viscosity:

  • Liquid outer core: Near-instant transmission, no accumulation
  • Solid mantle: Slow transmission, stress builds and stores
  • LLSVPs: Deflect flow like boulders in a river
  • Subducted slabs: Cold, dense - create downward drag channels
  • Plumes: Hot, buoyant - create upward pathways
  • ULVZs: Partial melt "puddles" that concentrate stress

Prediction Implications

When external drag aligns with internal structure, predictability improves:

HIGH predictability: External drag + slab alignment + channel pathway
MEDIUM predictability: External drag present but structure misaligned
LOW predictability: LLSVP blocking, no channel alignment

⏰ Regional Timing Signatures

Different regions show different timing signatures based on what the stress vector passes through. Volcanic regions respond rapidly; cold slab regions have delayed responses.

🔍 Testable Predictions

Volcanic Regions (Hot Path)

  • Expected response window: 6-48 hours
  • Lower kappa = less resistance to stress flow
  • Low sigma = minimal stress storage
  • High lambda = efficient transmission
  • Examples: Hawaii, Iceland, Yellowstone influence zones
Prediction: Events near Hawaii should correlate with lunar/solar triggers within 24-48 hours

Cold Slab Regions (Cold Path)

  • Expected response window: 1-4 weeks
  • Higher kappa = more resistance to stress flow
  • High sigma = significant stress storage
  • Low lambda = poor transmission (stress builds)
  • Examples: Farallon slab, Tethyan slabs, Pacific slab graveyard
Prediction: Deep events near subducted slabs should show 1-3 week delay from trigger alignment

LLSVP Channeling Effect

Regions above African or Pacific LLSVPs should show enhanced predictability when external drag aligns with the LLSVP margins. The "channeling" effect routes stress preferentially, creating more predictable pathways. Test: Compare hit rates for events above LLSVPs vs. away from LLSVPs.
